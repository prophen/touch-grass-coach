## Deadline and entry

The [challenge](https://dev.to/challenges/hacktoberfest-week1-2026-10-05) lists October 11, 2026 at 11:59 PM PDT as the deadline. Use its Submission Template link, keep `devchallenge` and `hf26challenge`, and include the live app, repository, and explanation of Gemma. The draft enters Best Use of Gemma. A deployed demo link is sufficient; a video is optional.
