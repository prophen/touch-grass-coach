# Outdoor trials

No firsthand outdoor trial has been recorded yet. Browser demonstrations do not count as outdoor evidence.

For each trial, record:

- Date and device/browser (no precise location needed).
- Screen hours, chosen duration, movement, and coaching tone.
- Actual generated mission.
- Whether the mission was understandable and possible in the selected setting.
- Elapsed time, including leaving and returning.
- Whether closing/reopening restored the task and completion earned one plant.
- What felt awkward, and any resulting app change.

Do not fill in observations until the mission has actually been tried.
